# Worker Council Assistant — User Quick Start Guide

## 📱 Get Started in 5 Minutes

This guide walks you through installing and using the Worker Council Assistant on your phone or computer.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Install the App (2 minutes)

### On Your Phone (Android or iPhone)

**Step 1:** Ask your works council for the app URL (e.g., `https://worker-council-assistant.onrender.com/`)

**Step 2:** Open the link in your browser

**Step 3:** Look for an **Install** button or prompt
- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** You'll see an install banner at the top — tap **Install**

**Step 4:** The app appears on your home screen like any other app ✅

### On Your Computer (Windows/Mac/Linux)

**Step 1:** Visit the URL in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended)

**Step 2:** Look for the **Install** icon in the address bar (looks like a small house or plus sign)

**Step 3:** Click it and confirm

**Step 4:** The app opens in its own window (like a desktop app) ✅

---

## ❓ How to Ask a Question (30 seconds)

### Using the App

1. **Open** the app on your phone/computer
2. **Type your question** in the text box (in German or English)
   - Example: *"Can my employer change my shift without asking the works council?"*
   - Beispiel: *"Wie wird ein Betriebsrat gewählt?"*
3. **Tap Ask** (or press Enter)
4. **Wait 3–5 seconds** for your answer
5. **Read the answer** — it includes § (section) numbers so you can verify it

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Do I need an internet connection?
**A:** Yes, you need internet to **ask questions** (the app sends your question to an AI). However, after your first visit, the app works **offline** for your **recent questions** and **sources**.

### Q: Is my question private?
**A:** Your question is sent to AI servers (Groq, Gemini, or OpenRouter) to generate an answer. The company does **not store or log** your questions. For very sensitive questions, ask your works council about on-premise deployment.

### Q: What language should I use?
**A:** German or English — **the app auto-detects**. Ask in whichever language you're comfortable with, and you'll get an answer in that language.

### Q: What if I don't understand the answer?
**A:** 
1. Click **Source Excerpts** to see the official law text
2. Click a **Follow-up Question** to ask for clarification
3. Ask a real lawyer for binding advice (this app is educational, not legal advice)

### Q: Can I share this with my colleague?
**A:** Yes! Share the URL with anyone:
- By email
- On WhatsApp / Messenger
- Print the URL and post it in the office
- Scan a QR code (if provided by your works council)

### Q: Where is my data stored?
**A:** Only on **your device** (browser storage). Your works council can see:
- How many questions were asked (total count)
- General topics people ask about (not specific content)

No personal data is stored on the server.

### Q: I found an error in an answer. What should I do?
**A:** 
1. Click **Source Excerpts** to see the official text
2. Compare the answer to the official law
3. If you find a real error, contact your works council with details

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **App won't install** | Try a different browser (Chrome works best) or ask your IT contact for help |
| **"All providers exhausted" error** | The app tried multiple AI providers and all were busy. Wait 5 min and try again. |
| **Answer is slow (> 15 sec)** | The AI provider is slow today. This is normal on free tier. Try again. |
| **Old answers appear** | Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) |
| **Can't see Recent questions** | Clear browser cache or open app in incognito/private mode |
| **Language detected wrong** | The app tries to guess. If it's wrong, ask your question more clearly or use keywords like "English:" at the start |

## 🎓 Best Practices

### ✅ DO
- Ask **clear, specific questions** ("What does the works council do in hiring decisions?")
- Click **Source Excerpts** to verify answers
- Use **Follow-up Questions** to drill deeper

- Use for **employment disputes** without consulting a lawyer
- Assume the law is **current** (laws change; check official sources)

## 📚 What Laws Are Covered?

This app has information about two German federal laws:

| Law | Full Name | Topics |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **BetrVG** | Betriebsverfassungsgesetz (Works Council Act) | Works council rights, elections, duties, co-determination, hiring, layoffs, working conditions |
| **BDSG** | Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (Data Protection Act) | Employee data rights, GDPR implementation in Germany, data subject requests, DPO responsibilities |

**Note:** This app covers German law for German companies. Other countries have different laws.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### ✅ What's Private
- Your questions are **not logged** permanently
- Your device stores **only your recent questions** (locally, not sent to server)
- **No cookies, no tracking, no analytics**
- **No data is sold**

### ⚠️ What You Should Know
- Questions are sent to **free AI providers** (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter) for processing
- Read their **privacy policies** if concerned
- For extremely sensitive questions, ask your works council about on-premise deployment

### 🔐 Tips
- **Don't type:** Passwords, personal identity numbers, confidential company secrets
- **Do type:** General questions about laws and procedures
- **Use incognito mode** on shared computers if you want extra privacy
